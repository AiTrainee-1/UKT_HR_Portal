1. WHAT THE HRMS IS USED FOR
=====================================================================================================

The UK Textiles HRMS is the single system that runs the people side of the company. It records and
controls:

  - Employee records           (who works here, department, designation, shift, HOD, salary details)
  - Attendance                 (fingerprint/biometric punches, GPS punches, shifts, late, half-day)
  - Leave and permissions      (leave, casual leave, late-in / early-out / middle permission)
  - Requests and approvals     (missing punch, on-duty, outpass, attendance corrections, resignation)
  - Payroll                    (staff monthly salary, production pay per shift, deductions, advances)
  - Gate control               (outpass QR, tea break QR, visitors)
  - Documents and salary slips (ID card, offer letter, salary slips)

The most important point for you: SALARY IS CALCULATED FROM THE ATTENDANCE AND REQUESTS IN THE HRMS.
If a punch is missing, a request is pending, or an approval is late, the salary of that employee can
be wrong or delayed. Your follow-up work protects the accuracy of payroll.


=====================================================================================================
2. WHY EVERYONE MUST FOLLOW THE PROCESS
=====================================================================================================

  - Payroll uses the HRMS only. Anything not recorded or not approved in the HRMS is treated as not
    happened (absent, late, no leave).
  - Every deduction (late, half-day, absent, loss of pay) must be explainable to the employee. The
    HRMS keeps the reason for each day. That only works if requests are raised correctly and on time.
  - Requests can only be raised for the CURRENT month (plus last month on the 1st and 2nd). If an
    employee waits, the system will refuse the request and HR has to correct it by a longer route.
  - A request waiting with an HOD is a request that cannot be finished. Late approvals delay payroll
    for the whole company at month end.
  - Everything is logged (who did it, when, with what comment). This protects the company and the
    employee in any dispute.

5. MOBILE APP OR EMPLOYEE WEB APP - WHICH ONE, AND WHEN
=====================================================================================================

Both apps are connected to the same data. Whatever is done in one is visible in the other. The rules
(dates allowed, approval route, wording) are the same in both.

USE THE MOBILE APP WHEN (it needs the phone's location, camera or QR):
  - GPS punch ("Attendance Request") - needs the phone's location
  - On-Duty punches - need location + selfie camera
  - Live tracking (if HR enabled it for the employee)
  - Showing the Digital ID Card QR at the gate (outpass and tea break)
  - HODs approving on the move (quick approve / reject)
  - Any time the employee is away from a computer

USE THE EMPLOYEE WEB APP WHEN (the employee or HOD is at a computer):
  - Longer forms: leave, permission, casual leave, missing punch, outpass
  - Reviewing attendance of the month on a larger screen
  - Downloading salary slips and documents
  - HOD reviewing many approvals together (the web Approvals page includes On-Duty approvals as well)

RULE OF THUMB: Anything involving location, camera or the gate = Mobile App. Everything else can be
done in either; do it wherever the person is comfortable, but do it the same day.

Important: attendance PUNCHES at the factory/office are on the fingerprint device; the apps are for
requests, checking and (where enabled) GPS punching. An employee must not depend on the app instead
of the device unless HR has told them to use GPS punch.

6. THE APPROVAL WORKFLOW (EMPLOYEE -> HOD -> HR)
=====================================================================================================

6.1 The standard route

  EMPLOYEE raises the request in the app
        |
        v
  HOD reviews and approves / rejects (in the app)
        |
        v
  HR gives the final approval (HR Portal) where the route requires it
        |
        v
  Employee is notified; attendance / balances / payroll update automatically

6.2 What the system is built with by default (HR can change it - see 6.4)

  REQUEST                                   BUILT-IN ROUTE
  ----------------------------------------  ----------------------------------------------------
  Leave                                     Employee -> HOD or HR (whoever acts first)
  Permission (Late-In / Early-Out / Middle) Employee -> HOD or HR (whoever acts first)
  Casual Leave                              Employee -> HOD or HR (whoever acts first)
  Gate Outpass                              Employee -> HOD or HR (whoever acts first)
  Missing Punch                             Employee -> HOD -> HR
  On-Duty (field visit)                     Employee -> HOD (optional) -> HR
  Resignation                               Employee -> HOD -> HR (HR can always reject)
  On-Duty punch verification (selfie/GPS)   Employee -> HR only (HR checks the photo and location)
  Attendance correction                     HR -> HOD (HR asks, the HOD must approve)
  Advance / loan                            Entered and approved by HR only
  Other requests (tickets)                  Employee -> HR

6.3 What HODs and the Coordinator must understand about the route

  - THE COMPANY EXPECTATION IS THAT THE CONFIGURED ROUTE IS FOLLOWED. HODs must not bypass it, hold
    requests back, or tell employees to "just ask HR directly". If the route is Employee -> HOD -> HR,
    HR cannot finish a request that is still waiting for the HOD.
  - Every request shows who it is waiting for: "Waiting for HOD" or "Waiting for HR". Use this to
    know whom to chase.
  - Leave balance is deducted at the FINAL approval, whoever gives it.
  - A rejection should always carry a reason in the comment. The employee receives the reason.
  - An HOD cannot approve their own request. A department head's own requests, and requests of
    employees who have no HOD at all, go to HR. Check the HOD list (section 11) so nobody is left
    without an approver.
  - Requests that are already waiting follow the route that is in force now. If HR changes the route,
    nothing is approved or rejected by the change itself.
  - If HR switches a request type OFF, employees cannot raise new ones ("...requests are switched off
    right now. Please contact HR.") but waiting ones can still be decided.

6.4 Check setting: where the live route is shown

  The HR Portal shows "Approval pipeline: Employee -> HOD -> HR" at the top of each request screen
  (Leave & Holiday, Requests, Casual Leave, Missing Punch, Outpass, Resignations, On-Duty approvals).
  The full set is in User Management -> Approval Workflow Control. Always follow what is shown there.

7. THE RULES EVERYONE MUST KNOW
=====================================================================================================

7.1 Shift timing and punches

  - Each employee is on a shift (start, end, grace minutes). Late and half-day are judged against
    THEIR OWN shift, not a company-wide clock time.
  - Staff normally punch 4 times a day: In, Lunch Out, Lunch In, Out. Two taps within 5 minutes count
    as ONE punch, so tapping twice does not "do" the next punch.
  - Production employees are paid per shift (segments such as first half, second half, extra time);
    the punches decide which segments count.
  - An employee with NO shift assigned is never marked late and has no timeline - HR must assign
    one. (Coordinator: look for these - section 10.)
  - Punch only for yourself. Never punch for another person.

7.2 What the first punch of the morning means (staff) - example 09:00 shift, 10 minutes grace

  FIRST PUNCH       WHAT IT IS                          WHAT THE DAY EARNS
  ----------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------------
  up to 09:10       On time                             Full day (1.00)
  09:11 - 10:10     LATE (late window 60 min)           Full day, and 1 late counted for the month
  10:11 - 11:10     Permission window (60 min)          With an approved Late-In permission: full
                                                        day, no late. Without it: quarter-shift
                                                        deduction (0.75)
  11:11 - 11:30     Extra minutes (20 min)              Quarter-shift deduction (0.75); a
                                                        permission does NOT cover these minutes
  after 11:30       First half missed                   Absent until a punch at/after 2:30 PM,
                                                        then Half Day (0.50)

  Check setting: the 60 / 60 / 20 minutes, the 0.25 deduction and the 2:30 PM second-half start are
  company settings (Settings -> Attendance). Each day's reason in the HR Portal tells which zone the
  employee fell in.

7.3 Late policy and deductions

  - Late-ins, early-outs and "excess" permissions all go into one monthly "late pool".
  - The first few in the pool each month are free (default allowance: 3). After that, the deduction
    slabs set in Settings -> Late Detection apply (for example 0.5 shift per extra late).
  - Late Detection can also warn employees on WhatsApp if HR has switched the alert on.

7.4 Permissions (Late-In, Early-Out, Middle One-Hour)

  - There are three types, 60 minutes each: Morning Late-In, Evening Early-Out, Middle One-Hour.
  - Employees must raise the permission request and get it APPROVED. Approval is what protects them.
  - Only the first 3 approved permissions in a month (default cap, all types together) are "Allowed".
    Any approved permission beyond the cap is "Overdue / Excess": it does not excuse anything and
    counts as one late occurrence. A rejected permission is "Not Allowed".
  - A permission cannot be duplicated for the same day and type.
  - Check setting: Early-Out detection can be switched on or off by HR (default off). Morning Late-In
    detection is on by default.

7.5 Leave

  - Full day, several days or half day (morning / afternoon).
  - Approved leave is marked on the attendance automatically and is not Loss of Pay (while balance
    lasts).
  - Absent with no request = absent / Loss of Pay (and "without permission" deduction where set).

7.6 Casual Leave

  - One casual leave per calendar month, counted by the month of the date requested.
  - The employee must have completed the minimum service (6 months). The app shows eligibility.

7.7 DATES THAT CAN BE REQUESTED  (this causes many follow-ups - read carefully)

  - Leave, Casual Leave, Permission and Missing Punch can only be requested for days in the CURRENT
    MONTH (1st to last day).
  - On the 1st and 2nd of a month, LAST month is still open (grace). From the 3rd it is closed.
  - A leave of several days must start AND end inside the month (split it if it crosses month end).
  - Missing Punch can never be in the future (latest date is today).
  - The system uses India date, so changing the phone clock does not help.
  - If a day is already closed, the employee cannot fix it. HR raises an Attendance correction in the
    HR Portal and the HOD must approve it. This is slow - so the rule for everyone is: DO IT THE SAME
    DAY OR THE NEXT DAY.

7.8 Missing punch

  - Employee reports the missed punch (date, which punch, reason). Route: Employee -> HOD -> HR.
  - Until approved, the day may show wrongly (absent / half day / late).

7.9 On-Duty (working outside)

  - Employee requests with destination -> HOD -> HR approves -> session becomes Active.
  - While active, punches are taken in the Mobile App with a selfie and GPS (up to 4 per day).
    HR verifies each punch. Fake/simulated GPS location is rejected and investigated.
  - The employee must tap "Mark as Done" at the end (or the 4th approved punch ends it).

7.10 Gate: Outpass and Tea Break

  - OUTPASS: request in the app BEFORE leaving during work time. After approval the app shows a QR.
    Security scans it when the employee goes out and again on return. Late returns and
    not-returned outpasses are reported.
  - TEA BREAK: no request and no approval. The employee's permanent QR (Digital ID Card in the Mobile
    App) is scanned at the gate on going out and again on coming back. HR sets the allowed minutes.
    Breaks over the allowed time are reported as overtime; no return scan within 60 minutes is
    reported as "not returned". A forgotten scan reverses the next scan and creates false long
    breaks - so ALWAYS scan both ways.

8. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES  (what / where / how / why)
=====================================================================================================

For each item: WHAT to do, WHERE (Mobile App = M, Employee Web App = W), WHY.

8.1 ATTENDANCE / PUNCH
  What : Punch on the fingerprint device at the correct times: In at shift start, Lunch Out, Lunch In,
         Out at shift end - all 4 punches. If HR has allowed GPS punch, punch inside the company area
         through the Mobile App ("Attendance Request").
  Where: Fingerprint device; GPS punch in M.
  Why  : Attendance is the base of salary. A missing punch can turn a full day into a half day or
         absent day. Late and early-out are judged from the punches.

8.2 FOLLOWING SHIFT TIMINGS
  What : Reach before shift start + grace. Know own shift in "My Shift". Do not leave before shift
         end. Do not punch on the shift of another group.
  Where: M or W -> My Shift.
  Why  : Late, half-day and early-out are measured against the employee's own shift. Repeated lates
         beyond the free allowance reduce salary.

8.3 CHECKING ATTENDANCE
  What : Check the Attendance calendar EVERY DAY (or at least every 2-3 days). Any day that looks
         wrong (absent, half day, late although on time) must be reported through the right request
         the same day.
  Where: M or W -> Attendance.
  Why  : Requests are only allowed in the current month (and the 1st-2nd of next). Errors found late
         cannot be corrected by the employee and delay salary.

8.4 LEAVE
  What : Apply BEFORE the leave (or the same day if unplanned). Choose the correct type, dates, and
         half-day option if needed, and write a clear reason. Stay inside the current month.
  Where: M or W -> Apply Leave. (Casual Leave: Casual Leave screen.)
  Why  : Approved leave is not Loss of Pay. Unapproved absence is. The HOD needs time to decide.

8.5 PERMISSION (including Late-In and Early-Out requests)
  What : Raise the permission request BEFORE the late arrival or early exit when possible (same day
         at the latest). Choose the right type: Morning Late-In, Evening Early-Out, Middle One-Hour.
         Wait for approval - do not assume.
  Where: M -> Permission, or W -> Permissions.
  Why  : An approved Late-In permission (within the monthly cap of 3) removes the late mark for
         arrivals inside the permission window. Without it the day can lose a quarter shift or
         count as late. Excess permissions beyond the cap do not protect the employee.

8.6 MISSING PUNCH
  What : The same day a punch is missed or the device failed, submit Missing Punch with date, the
         punch that is missing and the reason. Never for future dates.
  Where: M -> Missing Punch, or W -> Missing Punch.
  Why  : Without it the attendance stays incomplete. It must pass HOD and HR, so it needs time.

8.7 ON-DUTY
  What : Request On-Duty BEFORE leaving for work outside, with the destination. Start only after it
         is approved. Take every punch with the selfie and real location. Tap "Mark as Done" at the
         end. Never use fake-location apps.
  Where: M (needs location and camera).
  Why  : It makes outside work count as Present. HR checks each selfie and location; a fake location
         gets the punch rejected.

8.8 OUTPASS
  What : Request the outpass BEFORE leaving, with reason and time. Leave only after approval. Show
         the QR to security when going out and when coming back. Return on time.
  Where: M or W to request; M to show the QR.
  Why  : Leaving without it is unauthorised absence during working hours. Gate scans create the
         record of how long the employee was out.

8.9 TEA BREAK / TEA TIME
  What : Keep the Digital ID Card QR ready. Scan when going out for tea and scan again when
         returning. Stay within the allowed minutes.
  Where: M -> Digital ID Card, scanned at the gate.
  Why  : Tea break times are recorded and reviewed. A missing return scan makes the next scan wrong
         and shows false long breaks.

8.10 CHECKING REQUEST STATUS
  What : After submitting, open the request list and see its status. Each request shows who it is
         waiting for ("Waiting for HOD" / "Waiting for HR") and the step trail. If it waits more
         than a day, remind the HOD, then tell the Coordinator.
  Where: M or W -> the same screen where it was raised (Leave, Permission, Missing Punch, Outpass,
         Casual Leave) and Notifications.
  Why  : An approved request is what protects the day from deduction. A request nobody decides
         protects nothing.

8.11 FOLLOWING THE APPROVAL PROCESS / CORRECTLY SUBMITTING REQUESTS
  What : Raise every request in the app (never only by phone or verbal). Choose the correct type
         and date. Write the real reason. Do not submit duplicates. Do not ask HR to approve
         something the HOD has not yet seen unless the process allows it.
  Where: M or W.
  Why  : The route is fixed so that each person with authority sees the request, and everything is
         auditable.

8.12 CHECKING NOTIFICATIONS AND ALERTS
  What : Open Notifications daily. Read approval / rejection messages and rejection reasons. Act on
         them (resubmit correctly if needed). If WhatsApp alerts are on, respond to late / missing
         punch alerts the same day.
  Where: M or W -> Notifications; WhatsApp on the registered number.
  Why  : Decisions and alerts have time limits (the month window), so late reading means lost
         corrections.

8.13 CONTACTING HR FOR A SYSTEM OR LOGIN ISSUE
  What : Cannot log in, code not coming, account inactive, wrong profile detail, app not opening,
         attendance not showing -> use the Help screen: HR Department contact for login/account/app
         problems; Software / IT Support contact if the server or app cannot connect. Give employee
         code, what happened and the time.
  Where: M or W -> Help (contact details shown there).
  Why  : These cannot be fixed by the employee or HOD. Delay means missed request deadlines.

8.14 OTHER THINGS EMPLOYEES CAN DO (no deadline, but useful)
  - Download salary slips (M or W -> Salary)
  - View holidays, documents, own advance/loan status, company information
  - Check Profile and report any wrong detail to HR (employees cannot edit it)
  - Resignation (M or W -> Resignation) goes through HOD and HR

9. HOD RESPONSIBILITIES  (what / where / how / why)
=====================================================================================================

Where an HOD works: Mobile App -> Approvals tab, or Employee Web App -> Approvals page. HODs do not
use the HR Portal. They only see requests of the employees assigned to them (their department, plus
any individually assigned people).

9.1 REVIEWING AND DECIDING REQUESTS  (daily)
  What : Open Approvals at least TWICE a day (start of the day and end of the day).
         Recommended: decide every request on the same working day; never leave one past the next
         working day.
         Tabs: Leave | Permission | Casual Leave | Attendance | Missing Punch | Outpass |
         Resignation (On-Duty is in the Employee Web App Approvals page).
  Why  : Waiting requests block HR and payroll. Employees' days stay unprotected while they wait.

9.2 LEAVE REQUESTS
  Check: Is the date inside the month window? Is the team coverage acceptable? Is the reason
         genuine? Does it match what the employee told you?
  Do   : Approve or reject with a comment. Rejection must have a reason.

9.3 PERMISSION REQUESTS (Late-In, Early-Out, Middle)
  Check: Was the employee really late / leaving early on that date (compare with what you saw)? The
         permission type must match. Remember only the first 3 approved per month protect the
         employee; the rest count as late anyway.
  Do   : Approve genuine ones; reject false ones with reason.

9.4 LATE-IN REQUESTS AND EARLY-OUT REQUESTS
  These are the Permission types "Morning Late-In" and "Evening Early-Out". Handle as 9.3. Be
  consistent - the same rule for everyone in the team.

9.5 MISSING PUNCH
  Check: Was the employee present that day? Is the claimed punch time believable (shift timing)?
  Do   : Approve if true. It then goes to HR for the final approval. Reject if you cannot confirm.

9.6 ON-DUTY REQUESTS
  Check: Is the destination and purpose work-related, and did you send them?
  Do   : Approve or reject. HR then does the final approval and checks each selfie and GPS punch.

9.7 OUTPASS REQUESTS
  Check: Reason valid, time reasonable, work covered. Decide BEFORE the planned time - an outpass
         approved after the employee already left is useless.
  Do   : Approve or reject quickly (these are time-sensitive, within minutes).

9.8 ATTENDANCE CORRECTION (raised by HR)
  What : HR proposes a change to a day's attendance (for example when an employee could not request
         in time). You must approve or reject it.
  Check: Does it match what really happened? You are certifying it.
  Why  : It changes what payroll pays. Only you know the truth about your team's day.

9.9 CASUAL LEAVE AND RESIGNATION
  Casual Leave: check the single casual leave of the month is genuine.
  Resignation : the HOD review comes before HR; record the comment honestly and quickly.

9.10 FOLLOWING UP WITH EMPLOYEES WHEN CLARIFICATION IS REQUIRED
  What : If you need an explanation, ask the employee directly (phone / in person) BEFORE deciding,
         and write the outcome in the comment. Do not leave the request waiting while you "think".
  Why  : The comment is part of the record; HR and the employee see it.

9.11 MONITORING EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE, LATE ATTENDANCE AND MISSING PUNCHES
  Important: the HOD screens in the apps show APPROVALS, not a team attendance sheet. HODs therefore
  know about lates, absences and missing punches from (a) what they see in the shop floor / office,
  (b) the requests that arrive, and (c) the daily list the Processing Coordinator sends them.
  What : Each day, compare your team on the floor with the attendance list you receive. Tell
         employees to raise the missing punch / permission / leave the same day. Tell the
         Coordinator about any employee who is present but shows absent.
  Why  : Attendance errors must be corrected within the month window.

9.12 MONITORING PENDING REQUESTS
  What : Keep the Approvals tabs at zero. The tabs show counts. Anything left is yours.
  Why  : Your pending count is visible to HR (Pending Approvals Ageing, Approver Workload reports).

9.13 ESCALATING TO HR
  Escalate to HR (through the Coordinator, or directly) when: an employee in your list is not yours
  (wrong HOD assignment), a request type is blocked or switched off, an employee has no shift or
  wrong shift, suspected fake location or false punch, repeated misuse by one employee, you will be
  on leave and nobody covers approvals, or you cannot see a request an employee says they raised.

9.14 THE CONFIGURED ROUTE MUST BE FOLLOWED
  HODs must follow the approval route set by HR in the system. They must not approve outside it,
  ask employees to send requests to someone else, or sit on requests. If the HOD will be absent,
  inform HR so approvals can be reassigned (an inactive HOD approves nothing).

10. PROCESSING COORDINATOR - HOW TO FOLLOW UP
=====================================================================================================

What you need: an HR Portal login. HR decides which pages each login can open. If a page below is
missing from your sidebar, ask HR for VIEW access to it. You do not need edit access to follow up.

10.1 DAILY ROUTINE (Recommended timing - adjust to your shift times)

  A. START OF DAY, after the morning grace and extra minutes have passed (about 11:30 for a 09:00
     shift)
     1. HR Portal -> Dashboard: note Present Today, On Leave, Pending Approvals, Biometric Sync.
        If Biometric Sync shows an error or is old, tell HR / IT immediately: no punch means no
        attendance. (Report: Biometric Device Sync Health.)
     2. Reports -> "Daily Absentee / Leave List" for today: everyone absent. For each, check whether
        a leave / permission / On-Duty request exists. No request and no reason = call the employee.
     3. Reports -> "Late Coming Detail" (today): list of late arrivals. Check whether a Late-In
        permission was raised. If not, remind the employee to raise it today.

  B. MID-DAY
     4. Pending requests: Reports -> "Pending Approvals Ageing" (or the Leave & Holiday /
        Requests / Missing Punch / Casual Leave / Outpass screens). Note each pending request, how
        old it is and "Waiting for HOD" or "Waiting for HR".
     5. Contact each HOD with pending items (section 13 has the message).
     6. Outpass: pending outpass requests are time-sensitive - chase HODs first.

  C. END OF DAY, about 30 minutes after shift end
     7. Reports -> "Punch Exceptions" and "Daily Attendance Register": employees with missing
        punches (for example no Lunch In or no Out). Message each: raise Missing Punch today.
     8. Reports -> "Gate" group: "Outpass Not Returned", "Tea Break Exceptions" (overtime, not
        returned): list for HR.
     9. Reports -> "Shift Assignment Gaps" or "Employee Confo Details": employees with
        "Shift Assign = Not Assigned" - tell HR to assign a shift (without a shift, late and
        half-day cannot be judged).
    10. Send the HODs the day list (section 13.4).

  D. NEXT MORNING
    11. Re-check what you chased. Anything still open: second reminder; if the HOD is the blocker,
        escalate (section 12).

10.5 WHICH HOD APPROVALS ARE PENDING

  - Use Pending Approvals Ageing, grouped by approver.
  - To know WHO the HOD of an employee is: Reports -> "Employee Confo Details" (HR Reports group)

    joining and SHIFT ASSIGN ("Not Assigned" if none). Filter by Staff, Production or Other. Use it
    as your call list: employee -> HOD -> shift in one sheet. It needs Salary or Payroll access
    because it shows salary; if you cannot open it, ask HR for access, or use "Employee to HOD
    Mapping".

10.6 WEEKLY ROUTINE (Recommended - for example every Monday and Friday)

  - Late Coming Summary (Counts) for the month so far: employees who have crossed the free
    allowance; remind them and their HOD.
  - Permission Counts: employees at 3 permissions (cap reached - further ones do not protect them).
  - Missing Punch Frequency: employees who miss punches repeatedly.
  - Approver Workload & Turnaround: HODs with a backlog.
  - Shift Assignment Gaps and Employee to HOD Mapping: data problems for HR.
  - Casual Leave Eligibility & Usage if employees ask.

10.7 MONTH-END ROUTINE (the most important days)

  3 to 5 working days before month end:
    - Announce to employees and HODs: "Raise ALL pending leave / permission / missing punch requests
      now. Requests are only possible for this month (plus last month on the 1st and 2nd)."
    - Clear the full pending list with every HOD.
  Last working day:
    - Zero pending approvals is the target. Escalate whatever remains.
    - Check Biometric sync is complete up to the last day.
  The 1st and 2nd of the next month (grace days):
    - Last chance for employees to raise last month's requests. Chase every open item and every
      employee with an unexplained absent / missing punch.
  From the 3rd:
    - Last month is closed to employees. Anything missed must be fixed by HR as an Attendance
      correction (HR raises, the HOD must approve). Tell HR the list as early as possible on the
      3rd. Payroll for the month should only be generated after this is cleared.

12. WHEN TO ESCALATE TO HR
=====================================================================================================

  Escalate to HR (Recommended triggers):

  EMPLOYEE SIDE
    - Employee cannot log in, no WhatsApp code, account says inactive / not registered
    - Request refused because the date is closed (needs an Attendance correction)
    - Attendance wrong although the employee punched correctly (device / sync / wrong shift)
    - Suspected false GPS location, punching for someone else, or tea / outpass misuse
    - Repeated policy breaches by the same employee after reminders

  HOD SIDE
    - Request waiting more than 2 working days after two reminders
    - Outpass or Missing Punch left undecided while it matters
    - HOD on leave / not reachable and nobody can approve
    - Employee has no HOD, or the wrong HOD (Employee to HOD Mapping)
    - HOD refuses to follow the configured route

  SYSTEM SIDE
    - Biometric Sync error or no punches coming in
    - Unmatched Device IDs (person's fingerprint ID not linked to an employee)
    - Employees with no shift assigned
    - App cannot connect to the server -> Software / IT Support contact
    - Month-end: any pending approvals still open on the last working day

  HOW: give HR a short written list: employee code, name, issue, date, what you already did, and
  when. Attach the Excel report if useful.

14. COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
=====================================================================================================

Q. The employee says "the app will not let me pick that date".
A. Only the current month is allowed (plus last month on the 1st and 2nd). Missing Punch cannot be a
   future date. Past that, HR must correct attendance (HR raises, HOD approves).

Q. Can I approve a request in place of an HOD?
A. No. If the request is "Waiting for HOD", only the HOD (or HR, where the route says "HOD or HR")
   can decide. Chase the HOD, then escalate to HR.

Q. The request shows "Approved" but attendance still looks wrong.
A. Open the day in Attendance Search and read the reason. A permission beyond the monthly cap does
   not excuse the late. If still wrong, escalate to HR.

Q. Why was the employee marked half day although they came at 11:00?
A. The first-punch timeline (section 7.2). Late window, permission window, extra minutes and then
   the first half is missed. The reason is written on that day in the HR Portal.

Q. An employee says they punched but no punch is shown.
A. Check Punch Log for the day, Biometric Device Sync Health, and Unmatched Device IDs. If the
   punch is not there, the employee raises Missing Punch the same day; escalate device problems to
   HR / IT.

Q. Employee has no HOD.
A. Their requests go to HR. Tell HR to assign one (Employee to HOD Mapping / Employee Confo Details
   shows "Not Assigned").

Q. An HOD cannot see an employee's request.
A. The employee may not be assigned to that HOD, or the HOD's approval switch for that request type
   is off. Escalate to HR (User Management).

Q. Can employees punch with the app instead of the device?
A. Only if HR has enabled GPS punch for them, and only inside the company area. Otherwise the
   fingerprint device is the punch.

Q. What if the app says a request type is switched off?
A. HR has paused it in Approval Workflow Control. Ask HR; do not tell the employee to bypass it.

Q. Do WhatsApp alerts always go out for late / missing punch?
A. Only if HR has switched them on. Do not depend on them; your daily list is the safety net.

15. SETTINGS THAT CHANGE THE ANSWERS  (confirm with HR before relying on them)
=====================================================================================================

  - The live approval route per request type (User Management -> Approval Workflow Control).
  - The late window, permission window, extra minutes, quarter-shift deduction and second-half start
    time (Settings -> Attendance).
  - The free late allowance and the deduction slabs (Settings -> Late Detection).
  - The monthly permission cap (default 3) and whether Early-Out detection is on (default off).
  - Whether GPS punch and live tracking are enabled for a person.
  - Whether WhatsApp attendance alerts (late, absent, missing punch, 4-punch reminders) are on.
  - The allowed tea break minutes.
  - Which HR Portal pages and reports your login can open.
  The numbers in this guide are the system's standard values.
